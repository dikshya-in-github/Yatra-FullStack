About Yatra

Nepal's trusted digital gateway to the skies — built on trust, driven by technology, and made for every traveler.

Our Story

Soaring Higher Since Day One

Yatra was born from a simple observation — booking a domestic flight in Nepal shouldn't be complicated. What began as an academic dream has grown into Yatra: a modern, secure, and reliable flight booking platform built for the people of Nepal.

We continue to bring people and places together with care, comfort, and safety. From the Terai plains to the Himalayan peaks, we leverage modern technology to make domestic travel seamless and secure.

  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
  • Secure payments via eSewa integration
  • Instant booking confirmation by email
  • Real-time flight status tracking
