Yatra Takes Off: Nepal's Flight Booking Gets a Major Upgrade

Yatra today announced the launch of Yatra — a complete rebuild of its booking platform featuring secure eSewa payments, real-time flight status, group booking support, and instant email confirmations. The release marks the biggest milestone in the platform's history and reinforces its mission to make domestic travel in Nepal seamless and secure.
