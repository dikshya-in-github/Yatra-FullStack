Terms & Conditions

The ground rules for booking with Yatra — plain language, no surprises, matching the fare rules of our partner airlines.

Effective date: January 2026  ·  Domestic flights within Nepal

Book & Fly Domestic

Book scheduled flights with Buddha Air, Yeti Airlines, Shree Airlines and Sita Air across Nepal's domestic network. An e-ticket with your PNR is issued once payment succeeds.

15-Minute Booking Window

Seats you select are held for 15 minutes while you complete passenger details and payment. When the timer runs out the session expires and the seats are released.

01

About these terms

Yatra 2.0 is a booking platform for scheduled flights of Nepali domestic airlines — Buddha Air, Yeti Airlines, Shree Airlines and Sita Air. By completing a booking on Yatra you agree to these terms alongside the operating airline's own conditions of carriage.

Where these terms and an airline's fare rule differ, the operating airline's rule controls the flight; these terms govern how Yatra presents, processes and supports the booking.

02

Booking a flight

  • Passenger names must match the government-issued photo ID presented at check-in — enter them exactly as they appear on the ID.
  • Provide a reachable phone number and email; the e-ticket and any schedule changes go to the contact you enter.
  • Infants not occupying a seat travel on an adult's lap and pay no fare; each adult may accompany at most one lap infant.
  • Seats you select are held for 15 minutes between choosing a flight and completing payment; after that the hold expires and the session restarts.

A booking is confirmed only after payment succeeds. A pending booking that is never paid expires automatically and never appears in your booking history.

03

Fares & payment

  • Fares are quoted in NPR, per paying passenger (adults + children), and are shown per fare class with the applicable refundability.
  • Fares, schedules and availability can change until the booking is confirmed; the fare shown when you select a flight is the fare held for your 15-minute window.
  • Payments are accepted through eSewa. Other payment methods (cards, bank transfer, other wallets) are shown for demonstration and are not enabled yet.
  • Your transaction reference is your payment receipt; quote it together with your booking ID in any support request.
04

E-ticket & check-in

  • On successful payment Yatra issues an e-ticket with a booking reference (PNR) and ticket number — view, download and print it from the confirmation screen or My Bookings.
  • The e-ticket is your proof of booking; carry the same photo ID for every passenger at the airline's check-in counter.
  • Report at the airport before the airline's check-in cutoff — passengers arriving after cutoff are handled as no-shows under the airline's rules.
  • Baggage allowance is shown per flight card (checked 15 kg + 5 kg hand carry on typical domestic services, per the operating airline).
05

Changes & cancellations

Cancellation windows, fees and reschedule rules depend on your fare class and follow the operating airline's published fare rules — see the Refund Policy for the full fee table and the fee estimator.

  • Cancellations are processed against the fare class you booked; non-refundable fares still allow the unutilized airport tax (PSC) to be claimed.
  • If the airline cancels your flight, or delays it beyond one hour, you are entitled to a full refund regardless of fare class.
  • Refunds always return to the original payment method — wallets within 2–3 working days, cards/bank within 7–8 working days.
06

Liability

Yatra presents airline schedules and fares in good faith. The operating airline is responsible for the flight itself — including schedule changes, delays, diversions and carriage of passengers and baggage.

Yatra is not liable for losses arising from events outside its reasonable control (weather, air-traffic restrictions, airport closures, force majeure) or from passenger-side issues such as incorrect passenger details, missed check-in cutoffs or invalid travel documents.
