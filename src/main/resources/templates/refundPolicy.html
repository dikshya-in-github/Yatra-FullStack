Good to Know

Rescheduling & No-Shows

Rescheduling

  • Change your flight up to 2 hours before departure, subject to seat availability.
  • A reschedule charge applies; if your fare tier is sold out, you pay the fare difference to upgrade.
  • Cancel a rescheduled ticket later? Charges apply based on the original fare class, and reschedule fees are non-refundable.

No-Show

  • Miss the airline's airport check-in/reporting deadline and you're marked a no-show.
  • No-show tickets are not refundable for fare or fuel surcharge.
  • Unutilized Passenger Service Charge (airport tax) stays claimable on request.

When You Always Get 100% Back

If the airline cancels your flight (weather, technical, or operational), every fare class is fully refundable — no cancellation fee. If your flight is delayed more than 1 hour and you're at the airport, you can cancel on the spot and request a full refund.

Airline cancellation → full refund Delay over 1 hour → full refund on request PSC/airport tax → always claimable
Step by Step

How to Request a Refund

From your Yatra dashboard — no calls, no queues.

1. Open Your Booking

Log in and go to Ticket Status → select the booking you want to cancel.

2. Cancel Before the Cutoff

Tap Cancel Booking at least 2 hours before departure. The applicable fee for your fare class is shown before you confirm.

3. Get Instant Confirmation

A cancellation reference is generated and emailed to you immediately — keep it for tracking.

4. Receive Your Refund

Refunds go back to your original payment method — eSewa/Khalti in 2–3 working days, cards/bank in 7–8 working days.
