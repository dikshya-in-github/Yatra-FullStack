Tickets

The e-tickets Yatra has issued — the PNR and ticket number a customer prints or downloads from their booking. This page is read-only: a ticket is issued automatically when a payment settles, so the only thing you can do here is look one up. The Status column shows the ticket's own state, which is tracked separately from the booking it belongs to — to cancel a booking, use Bookings.
